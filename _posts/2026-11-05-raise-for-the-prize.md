The capability of considering a problem a way to generate wealth from is not new. Since the [origins of wealth](https://books.google.pl/books?id=eUoolrxSFy0C&hl=pl&source=gbs_book_other_versions_r&cad=1) we are trying to attribute value to objects. [SKUs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stock_keeping_unit) as a way to define and categorize items make our life easier, day by day, but also there are a lot of implicit rules out there that rule us all.

These days, I'm starting to believe the prize of being "there" might not be the best nor the most accurate outcome, except you are the one trying to rule us all.

![A person climbing a long staircase, reflected in the walls](https://blog.ignaciobrasca.com/img/posts/2026-11-05-raise-for-the-prize/stairs.jpg)

Photo by [Joseph Akbrud](https://unsplash.com/photos/person-climbing-stairs-3GX4PJ-qces) on Unsplash.

It seems economics are not needed, at least in theory, when you get immense funding because of your fundamentals which are stale for nowadays markets.

Right now, goals are being measured on things optimized to be against humanity, making workplaces always a threat to us.

Work fast, work more, work for the sake of it.

Waste energy.

Be relevant to the models, for them to be none to you.

Intelligence was always a commodity for us, the plurality, the workers. They extract the value from us without us extracting value from them.

But, who are they? Why?

Just execute. Which goals? Who knows.

Don't express yourself.

We hired you to act upon. Not against.
