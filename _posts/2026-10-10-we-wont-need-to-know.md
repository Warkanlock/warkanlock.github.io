I've been working in a heavy agentic workflow lately, and I foresee two states of what we are doing.

Barebones: enough architecture for us as a species to extract data and act on behalf of the commander.

And on the other side: problems utterly complex to scrape from.

The first to fall will be authentication. If everything is open, there is no one left to prove yourself to.

Then the web. Boxes in the shadow, communicating through obscure protocols optimized for machines, not us. Even a naive version of it, [MCP](https://modelcontextprotocol.io), skyrocketed, so the need and the adoption are clearly there. [A2A](https://a2a-protocol.org) is the horizontal twin: agent to agent instead of agent to tool.

There will be no network but a market. A minimalist global version of [Gemini](https://www.glukhov.org/post/2025/10/gemini-protocol/), the grandchild of [Gopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gopher_(protocol)), where agents won't even interact with us. It will be there for us to consume from, a GPS of knowledge, a universal standard, but with no need to connect yourself to it. You will already be there.

Not your name, not your identity. A rasterization of all your bits of information, chained together in a sequence only you have.

Decision makers will not look at landing pages. Insights will be exposed directly, for autonomous pointers, in a `.txt`. Like [llms.txt](https://llmstxt.org), but `insights.txt`: readable as data, not as content or usability.

It's the end of human-interface. One project points in this direction: [Ink & Switch](https://www.inkandswitch.com/essay/malleable-software/) and their malleable software, tools anyone can reshape with minimal friction instead of locked-down appliances. It feels like the right time for it: the sequence of bytes stops being a dead file and starts behaving like part of a living organism.

![Paul Dirac at the blackboard](https://blog.ignaciobrasca.com/img/posts/2026-10-10-we-wont-need-to-know/dirac.jpg)

As Dirac mentioned in his 1939 lecture, [The Relation Between Mathematics and Physics](https://www.billtoole.net/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/334419691-Paul-Dirac-The-Relation-Between-Mathematics-and-Physics.pdf):

> "It seems to be one of the fundamental features of nature that fundamental physical laws are described in terms of a mathematical theory of great beauty and power, needing quite a high standard of mathematics for one to understand it."

We want to be there. We want to be present in the loop, part of the node that orchestrates the rest. But we are being displaced from the conversation.

We should take the opportunity: think outside the box, and take on better problems we can finally tackle.

We won't go back. Not unless energy and infrastructure logistics, turbines, steam engines, all of it, are not sufficient. The [IEA counts](https://memeburn.com/ai-electricity-consumption-statistics-2026-and-power-demand/) data centres at 485 TWh in 2025, around 1.5% of global electricity, doubling by 2030.

If I had a billion dollars right now, I would invest in elevators, fire and maintenance, HVAC.

We won't need to know. At least not this, not anymore. To leave space for other problems, and make humanity a better hive.
